The three most-used DaF course books for adults teach essentially the same A1/A2 grammar core in nearly the same order. They differ mostly in themes and characters. Where they diverge, this plan follows Menschen, because that's what your university uses.

> Limitation: hueber.de and goethe.de were not reachable from the build environment, so the A1.2–A2.2 lesson **titles** are not Menschen's exact titles. Once you upload the Menschen A1.2 / A2 books, lessons 13–48 get remapped 1:1 to the book's Lektionen. The grammar and topics won't change much.

## 2. Grammar progression (the backbone)

**A1.1** (Menschen A1.1, L1–12)
1. Present tense, sein/haben, W-questions, verb position 2 (L1–2)
2. Yes/no questions, ja/nein/doch, mein/dein, vowel-change verbs (L3)
3. Definite article, er/es/sie for things (L4)
4. ein/kein, nicht vs. kein (L5)
5. Plural, accusative (den/einen/keinen) (L6)
6. können + Satzklammer (L7) · inversion, am/um + clock time (L8) · mögen/möchten, compounds (L9)
7. Separable verbs (L10) · Perfekt with haben (L11) · Perfekt with sein, im + month/season (L12)

**A1.2** (L13–24): dative after mit/zu/bei/von/aus/nach · Wechselpräpositionen + dative (Wo?) · müssen/wollen · all possessives · Präteritum war/hatte · ordinal numbers & dates · imperative · sollen · accusative pronouns · dative pronouns + dative verbs (gefallen, gehören …) · dürfen, man · und/aber/oder/denn

**A2.1** (L25–36): Präteritum of modal verbs · weil · Wechselpräpositionen + accusative (Wohin?), stellen/stehen etc. · dass, wenn · adjective endings (definite, indefinite) · comparative/superlative · reflexive verbs · indirect questions (ob/W-) · verbs with prepositions · Konjunktiv II (würde/hätte/könnte) · vor/seit/nach

**A2.2** (L37–48): zu-infinitive · deshalb/trotzdem · wo(r)-/da(r)- · passive present (receptive) · relative clauses (nom./acc.) · adjective endings dative / zero article · Genitiv-s, von + dative · dative + accusative objects · Futur I (werden) · sollte (advice) · clause review

## 3. Exam coverage (Goethe)

Every lesson has a **Prüfung** tab with tasks in real exam format, and the **Prüfung** page collects them by skill.

| Skill | Start Deutsch 1 (A1) | Goethe A2 | Where it's trained |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hören | 3 Teile, ~20 min: dialogues (2×), announcements (1×), phone messages (2×) | 4 Teile, 30 min | `dialogue` + `listen` tasks (TTS, play limit noted) |
| Lesen | 3 Teile, 25 min: e-mails R/F, ads a/b, signs R/F | 4 Teile, 30 min | `tf`, `mc` reading tasks |
| Schreiben | Formular (5 gaps) + ~30-word message (3 points) | SMS + e-mail (~30–40 words) | `form` (auto-checked) + `write` (checklist + model) |
| Sprechen | Sich vorstellen · Fragen mit Wortkarten · Bitten | Fragen zur Person · Über sich erzählen · Gemeinsam planen | `speak` (mic transcript + keyword check + model audio) + audio drill |

## 4. Method

* **Spaced repetition (FSRS-4.5, target retention 90 %).** Every word (with article), every grammar gap and every drill sentence becomes a card. Production first: you see English, you produce German. Recognition gets trained by reading and listening anyway.
* **Pimsleur-style audio drill.** Anticipation (answer before the voice), graduated-interval recall inside the session (5 s → 25 s → 2 min → 10 min → 30 min, reset on a miss), backward build-up for new sentences, old lessons mixed back in.
* **Interactive textbook.** Each lesson: Lernziele → Wortschatz (audio, gender colour code) → Grammatik (tables you can click to hear) → Redemittel → Übungen (gap, order, sort, match, MC, dictation, spelling, reading, listening, form, writing, speaking) → Audio-Drill → Prüfung.

## 5. Time budget (be honest with yourself)

* Weekdays: 30–60 min → 10′ reviews · 15′ drill · 20′ new pages · 10′ exam task
* Weekends: 90–120 min → finish the week's lessons + one Sprechen and one Schreiben task
* Total ≈ 105 h in-app + uni classes. Goethe's estimate for A1+A2 is ~150–200 h of instruction. That's tight but doable. It does not survive skipped weeks.
